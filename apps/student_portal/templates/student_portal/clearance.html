How Clearance Works
  1. Submit your clearance request through this page.
  2. Each department reviews its own records (library fines, fee balance, equipment, etc.).
  3. Departments mark you as "Cleared" once all obligations are settled.
  4. Once all departments clear you, the Registrar issues your clearance certificate.
  5. Your clearance certificate is required to collect results slips, report cards, and transcripts.
Clearance processing may take 3–5 working days. Contact the relevant department directly if an item remains pending after 5 days.
